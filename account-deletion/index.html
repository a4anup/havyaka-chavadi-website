Account deletion

Updated 21 October 2026

Delete your account in the app: Me → Account → Delete Account → confirm. Signing out does not delete your account.

If you cannot access the app, email hi@havyakachavadi.in from your registered email with the subject Account deletion request. We will verify ownership before processing the request. Never include an OTP or password.

Email an account deletion request

What account deletion removes

Your sign-in account, registration, survey history and profile photo are deleted. Katte thoughts/messages are redacted. Limited audit records and provider backups may be retained as described in the privacy policy. Deletion cannot be undone.
