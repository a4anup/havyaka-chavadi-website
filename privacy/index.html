Privacy policy

Updated 21 October 2026

We use your details for registration, membership verification and communications you request. Data is not sold. Authorized reviewers and service providers receive limited access for verification, OTP delivery and hosting. Addresses and contacts are private. Gurikkara details are optional. You can edit your profile and survey or delete your account. Minimal admin audit records may be retained for security and lawful obligations. Launch emails are optional and can be switched off in Edit Profile.

Information we use

The app collects your name, email, international phone number, address, optional profile photo, Valaya details and interest/pricing survey answers. Email ownership is confirmed using an emailed code. Phone numbers remain unverified. Membership approval is a separate review.

Your choices

Location access is optional and foreground-only, requested after you choose Use current location. Using the map shares the selected coordinates with OpenStreetMap; your device’s geocoder may process them to suggest an address. Coordinates stay in memory and are not saved with your registration. Only the address you review and submit is stored. Private survey revisions preserve annual and monthly answers separately and are removed when your account is deleted.

Access and service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database, private photo storage and real-time services. Resend delivers authentication emails. Registration details are accessible to you and authorized reviewers. Optional foreground location helps enter an address only when requested; the app does not require background location.

Katte conversations are private to eligible participants and remain as read-only memories after closure. Deleting your account removes your authored messages and anonymizes your seats. Reports and moderation records may be retained for safety. Optional notifications use Expo, Apple or Google; message contents are excluded. Notification history is kept for up to 90 days.

Deletion and retained records

Deleting your account removes your registration, survey history and profile photo. Chavadi Katte authored content is redacted as part of deletion. Limited security/audit records and service-provider backups may remain for security or lawful obligations. Contact us with retention questions.
