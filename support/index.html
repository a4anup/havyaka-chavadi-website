Help & support

Updated 21 October 2026

For sign-in, registration, Valaya review, safety reports or account deletion, email our support inbox. Describe the issue and include your app version if available. Never send passwords, verification codes or identity documents by email.

How does email sign-in work?

Enter your email and select Send OTP. Enter the six-digit code from your inbox within 10 minutes. You can request another code after 60 seconds. Successful verification signs you in or starts registration. Phone verification is not required.

Do I need to verify my phone number?

V0.1 collects your phone number with a country code in basic details, without phone verification. Phone login and phone OTP are disabled. One phone number can be linked to only one email/account.

What does Under verification mean?

Your registration has been saved, but community membership approval is still pending. Email or phone checks do not award the membership badge. An authorized reviewer handles membership decisions. Open Me → Refresh status to fetch the latest status.

Can I change my interests? Does pricing mean I am paying?

You can update your three selected interests from Home or Me, and edit your pricing preference under Me. The pricing question is a survey only. Submitting it does not purchase a subscription or charge you.
